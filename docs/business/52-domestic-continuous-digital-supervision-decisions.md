# Domestic continuous quality supervision — approved Business choices, 2026-10-09

Status: Approved *scope-level business directions*; Domestic Business Gate remains OPEN. This is not a technical implementation contract.

## RON-DEC-012 — continuous supervision instead of routine reinspection
The user confirmed continuous, documented Ronas supervision from the start of household production to harvest. If the supervision evidence is complete, credible, and shows compliance with applicable food-safety and quality criteria, routine repeat inspection solely at listing time is not required. Missing, contradictory or inadequate evidence does not establish quality; applicable mandatory independent inspection or testing is not waived. Producer permission to publish remains a separate requirement under RON-DEC-011.

## RON-DEC-013 — digital-first supervision method (choice 2)
The user chose option 2 of the supervision-method question: Ronas follows cultivation using producer-submitted **information, photographs and reports** as the routine digital monitoring channel, rather than routine in-person visits. This decision does not approve automatic image diagnosis, automatic quality certification, AI selection, an inspection frequency, an evidence-retention period, or any particular software stack. Mandatory independent physical tests/inspections and credible handling of missing or disputed data remain open.

## Existing commitments

- RON-DEC-010: producer determines or confirms the final price; Ronas may suggest but not impose prices.
- RON-DEC-011: surplus can become a private draft automatically; public listing requires both explicit producer approval and verified applicable product quality.
