**Scope:** This is an opt-in, read-only structural preparation packet for a *future* independently qualified Ronas specialist. ## API and authority

`GET /api/v1/agronomy/assigned-drafts/{ref}/review-handoff-fingerprint` is mounted **only** when the existing LOCAL/TEST `SqliteSyntheticExpertQuestionLedger` is explicitly configured. A correctly signed Keycloak `agronomy_expert` role and current exact DOMESTIC case assignment are mandatory; the software role **never** establishes professional competence. Access is 401 for unauthenticated, 404 for unauthorized/revoked/wrong engine, generic 503 for unavailable/tampered evidence/current source catalogue. No POST, PATCH, PUT, DELETE, accepted-state or expert-decision command is added.

The same transaction verifies the append-only case, household input, expert question, household answer and expert receipt-sighting histories, with exactly **one successful read audit** (`AGRONOMY_SYNTHETIC_REVIEW_HANDOFF_FINGERPRINT`). It reads *only* already-verified stored input version/manifest, question receipts, household reply receipts, specialist sighting receipts and bound proposal fixture. A current proposal must pass the existing exact-case/current-input structural check. A pinned scientific-source candidate fixture must pass the separate source catalogue equality check; unavailable, missing, amended-rights or changed-science candidate pointers **fail closed**. Without a separately pinned catalogue, the source remains `NO_SEPARATE_SOURCE_CATALOGUE`, not verified.

## Reproducible, non-authorizing fingerprint

A versioned, domain-separated canonical SHA-256 digest is recomputed from the exact synthetic case/version, current input digest/revision, model/proposal/stage **fixture** identifiers and digests, source-catalogue-check marker, every historical expert question's verified receipt and version, and linked owner-response/receipt-sighting digests. The returned `context_fingerprint_sha256` is an **ephemeral structural identity only**; it is **not** a digital signature, durable retention or future approval token. It is stable under repeated GETs with unchanged data because **READ audit events do not feed back into its hash**, and changes whenever a question/owner response/sighting or input, proposal, model or source fixture identity changes. A historic fingerprint has no authority to restore stale input or a former proposal.

Exactly three independent canonical tracks expose nonprivate question counts, household response receipt counts, specialist sighting counts, latest question revision, and a limited structural fixture-binding indicator. Source-catalogue absence keeps binding false; no actual source bytes/rights or real scientific review is claimed even when pointers match. The fingerprint carries no household reply/evidence refs, question reason/evidence refs, raw scientific text/source licensing docs, run/model identifiers or crop/stage instructions in its returned JSON. All purpose-consent, professional qualification, source/service/training rights, model execution, plan acceptance and new advice fields are **false**, and all tracks remain `NOT_REVIEWED`.

The output does not invent real required input fields, agronomy rules, plant/crop instructions, review criteria, retention periods or decision thresholds. The approved review decision process and human assignment/qualification requirements remain separate and absent. No Stage/QA Gate/Release/Production, merge, or Ready for Review; both PRs Draft/Open/Unmerged and Domestic #2, Export #3, Finance #4 OPEN. SQLite is LOCAL/TEST only.
