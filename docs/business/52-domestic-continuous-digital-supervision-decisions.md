# Domestic continuous quality supervision — approved Business choices, 2026-10-09

Status: Approved *scope-level business directions*; Domestic Business Gate remains OPEN. This is not a technical implementation contract.

## RON-DEC-012 — continuous supervision instead of routine reinspection
The user confirmed continuous, documented Ronas supervision from the start of household production to harvest. If the supervision evidence is complete, credible, and shows compliance with applicable food-safety and quality criteria, routine repeat inspection solely at listing time is not required. Missing, contradictory or inadequate evidence does not establish quality; applicable mandatory independent inspection or testing is not waived. Producer permission to publish remains a separate requirement under RON-DEC-011.

## RON-DEC-013 — digital-first supervision method (choice 2)
The user chose option 2 of the supervision-method question: Ronas follows cultivation using producer-submitted **information, photographs and reports** as the routine digital monitoring channel, rather than routine in-person visits. This decision does not approve automatic image diagnosis, automatic quality certification, AI selection, an inspection frequency, an evidence-retention period, or any particular software stack. Mandatory independent physical tests/inspections and credible handling of missing or disputed data remain open.

## RON-DEC-014 — reporting at product-specific cultivation-plan stages (choice 1)
**APPROVED — reporting cadence model, Domestic household cultivation only.** The user explicitly approved option 1: the producer submits information, photographs and reports **at defined stages of the cultivation plan for the relevant product**. Reporting is therefore **stage-based, not a uniform fixed-time schedule**. The required milestones, exact evidence at each milestone, stage owner/approval, what counts as a complete or authentic report, late/missing reports, plan changes and exceptional reports **are not approved** and must be derived later from a reviewed product-specific cultivation plan and applicable requirements.

**Boundary:** This is a Business reporting principle, not a mandated image upload at every stage, a reporting algorithm, a numerical frequency, an automatic AI quality decision or software implementation. It complements RON-DEC-012 continuous documented supervision and RON-DEC-013 digital-first supervision; it does not automatically establish quality, bypass applicable inspection obligations or permit publication without both independent RON-DEC-011 conditions.

## RON-DEC-015 — AI proposes, qualified expert reviews and approves cultivation plans (choice 2)
**APPROVED — Business authority boundary for Domestic household cultivation plans.** The user chose option 2: **AI produces a proposed product-specific cultivation plan, including candidate reporting stages; a qualified Ronas agricultural expert reviews the proposal and must explicitly approve it before the plan or stages become authoritative for the producer.** The expert can amend or reject a proposal; an unapproved proposal is not an operative cultivation plan or quality approval. This is consistent with RON-DEC-014, which makes reports dependent on the stages of the *approved* product-specific plan.

**Not approved:** model/provider/family, AI training or deployment method, source dataset, data rights/consent, knowledge sources, actual crop/stage list, milestone frequency/content, expert appointment and qualification criteria, evaluation threshold, autonomous plan promotion, automatic food safety/quality pass, price setting or sale publication. Any required legally recognized inspections remain mandatory. No code or Technical admission is authorized.

## RON-DEC-016 — household-specific AI-proposed cultivation plans (choice 2)
**APPROVED — Business personalization direction for Domestic household cultivation only.** The user chose option 2 for personalization: **AI drafts a distinct cultivation plan for each household, considering that household's climate, available cultivation space, facilities and circumstances; the qualified Ronas expert must explicitly review and approve that specific household plan.** The expert can amend or reject it. RON-DEC-015 established the AI-proposal/human-approval separation; RON-DEC-016 establishes the **household-specific scope** of each proposal. RON-DEC-014 stage-based reports refer only to the stages of that household's expert-approved plan, not generic AI output.

**Approval boundaries:** These are *candidate input categories*, not permission to collect, use, share, retain or train on any household information. Sources/accuracy/consent/privacy for climate/location/space/facilities and crop photos/reports, scientific plant-specific constraints, required evidence at each stage, expert qualifications, changes to plans, AI model/benchmark/runtime and exception handling remain OPEN. Approval of the plan does **not** auto-certify safety, grant marketplace publication or authorize payment; RON-DEC-011's producer-publication approval and independently valid quality evidence are still required. No API, storage design, ML implementation or Production is authorized.

## RON-DEC-017 — household-supplied information with informed consent (choice 1)
**APPROVED — limited Domestic personalized crop-plan input-source direction.** The user explicitly chose option 1: **each household supplies the information needed for its own personalized cultivation plan, knowingly and with consent**. This complements RON-DEC-016, which identifies climate, growing space, available facilities and household circumstances as possible tailoring dimensions. It does **not** approve automatic data enrichment from third parties, a mandated questionnaire or any specific fields, and does **not** imply permission to use the information for AI training, unrelated analytics, other households, Export or outside purposes.

**Unresolved separate data contract:** exact necessary versus optional information, purpose-specific notice and consent collection/record, who may access the data, verification and correction, handling missing/inaccurate details, withdrawal, retention/deletion, sharing, legal obligations and separate rights for any future training dataset. Information voluntarily supplied does not certify accuracy or approve a crop plan: the Ronas expert must independently approve each proposed household plan (RON-DEC-015/016). Public produce listing still requires the producer's separate publication authorization and applicable quality evidence (RON-DEC-011).
