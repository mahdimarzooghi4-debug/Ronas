In this opt-in mode, the guard opens the two files using the recovery-only `open_existing` constructors. It checks `SqliteLocalAuthorityCheckpoint.assert_current(journal)` before reading *any* owned candidate snapshots, again when preparing a scoped vector, and immediately before recording a successful case read. The guarded path does not accept arbitrary caller-provided witness callbacks alongside the journal-backed mode; all three witness generations come from the same verified journal-owned typed synthetic state. The journal-owned case-assignment Boolean must be TRUE in addition to the original signed Keycloak `agronomy_expert` and separately checked live exact-case grant. The scoped specialist evidence registry and (where a scientific source basis is configured) the current science candidates also come from the same journal-owned snapshot. The output remains redacted; `NOT_REVIEWED` and false authorization flags remain unchanged.

If checkpoint missing, unpinned, corrupt, mismatched, restored, different journal instance or changed between observations, the guarded read fails as sanitized 503 **before successful READ_ALLOWED audit**, without any implicit reinitialization or `pin_current`. If the checkpoint is explicitly updated after a local synthetic assignment revocation, access is 404; a subsequent explicitly pinned synthetic regrant revives only case-reading eligibility, and the old versioned fingerprint is still rejected 409 (never professional qualification or plan approval). The witness generation must not be inferred, reset or fabricated.

**Limit:** These are multiple bounded checks, not a distributed atomic transaction across journal, sidecar, case ledger or independent real providers. A change after the final check remains possible; a coordinated rollback of both local files can still appear internally consistent. The sidecar is not a secure external anchor. Local optimistic SHA comparison is not a review decision or permit. The Business gates remain OPEN and PRs Draft/Open/unmerged.
