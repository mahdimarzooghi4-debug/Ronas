## Why broad open-data labels do not settle Ronas use rights

| Candidate | Relevant official terms, not reuse approval | What Ronas cannot yet infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Domestic — AGROVOC** | [AGROVOC FAQ](https://aims.fao.org/standards/agrovoc/faq), [Access](https://aims.fao.org/standards/agrovoc/access-agrovoc), [FAO Maintenance](https://www.fao.org/agrovoc/index.php/maintenance) contain **different referenced CC BY licence versions** (CC BY IGO 3.0 vs CC BY 4.0); FAO languages and other contributor languages have different ownership. | A vocabulary is **not** an agronomy knowledge base, crop-specific treatment standard, scientifically reviewed AI training dataset, or automatic commercial-use authorization. Resolve exact distributed version, language, attribution and rights with authority. |
| **Domestic — FAO publications** | [Publications rights](https://www.fao.org/publications/about-fao-publishing/permissions/) and [FAO general terms](https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/) distinguish noncommercial default use, specific content licensing and third-party components. | Reading or citing a publication does **not** grant broad commercial adaptation/translation or training permission; qualified local expert and crop applicability remain separate. |
| **Export — FAOSTAT / corporate statistical DBs** | [Official database terms](https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/db-terms-of-use/en): default CC BY 4.0 where no different dataset metadata applies, **plus restrictions**, attribution and no FAO endorsement or enterprise-product promotion; third-party exceptions. | Neither licensing a dataset nor redistributing extracted statistics in an actual Ronas report is approved from a generic database landing page. |
| **Export — World Bank data** | [Data Catalog licenses](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses), [official dataset terms](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/legal/terms-conditions/datasets), [FAQ](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/faq): default CC BY 4.0 on qualifying data but third-party or other-license exceptions must be checked. | No exact country/product dataset is selected, versioned or permitted for Ronas's actual intended report; attribution and transformation details unknown. |
| **Export — UN Comtrade** | [Official use policy](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/policy-on-comtrade-data-use/), [redistribution guidance](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/re-dissemination-of-data/), [license agreement](https://comtradeplus.un.org/LicenseAgreement): internal use and public redistribution/commercial service are distinct, with specific exceptions/license terms. | Use-case-specific legal review is required; apparent differences between summary pages and agreement cannot be read as blanket permission. No public hosting, scraping, API ingestion, paid re-dissemination or buyer claims now. |

**Mandatory source evidence before moving a candidate to actual work:** exact source/dataset identity + immutable publication/metadata version; actual product–destination or crop-specific scientific question; data ownership/third-party exceptions; purpose/user audience (internal vs public/commercial vs AI training); an authoritative permission/licence version and attribution plan; a named authorized rights reviewer; any additional regional/legal limitations; and a signed scoped Business decision. Store no actual source data, household data or credentials through this screening.

**Independent technical/readiness boundary:** a public link is not a signed source-rights certificate, a scientific standard, an actual qualified expert, an accepted research report, or authority to train Ronas's internal AI. The five candidates in `docs/business/official-source-rights-screening.json` are **not approved** and not downloaded. D1-A and E0-A remain conditional Business candidates; actual operations/transactions/PSP remain blocked.
